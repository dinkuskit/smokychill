# Herbal Ice — LegitScript preparation

Status: draft preparation only. Nothing in this document publishes a product,
claims certification, or replaces labeling counsel.

## Factual product description

> Herbal Ice is a non-ingestible cooling topical balm formulated with menthol
> and camphor and made with CBD isolate. It is sold as a 4 oz jar on
> smokychill.com. It is manufactured in-house by Smoky Product Company.
> Intended use: topical skincare. The catalog is intended to contain only
> LegitScript-certified products.

## Approved storefront language

- The 1,000 mg reference formula uses menthol (10%) and camphor (5%).
- 4 oz jar available in 1,000 mg, 5,000 mg, and 10,000 mg CBD strengths.
- The 1,000 mg reference formula is documented; higher-strength formulas are
  pending formulation testing and do not use the 1,000 mg percentages.
- Topical use only; not for ingestion.
- Apply to skin as desired; wash hands after application; avoid eyes and
  mucous membranes; keep out of reach of children.
- Ingredient list is included on the product page and in the CMS draft seed.

## Blocked before publication or advertising

- UPC/GTIN (none has been issued; it remains optional and is never invented).
- Final brand line on the jar.
- Product and application photography.
- Batch COA and confirmation of the label's CBD/THC statements. Until a COA
  exists, do not say “THC-free” or “0 THC”.
- Verify the sender name `SMCLLC` in the borrowed shipping policy before launch.
- OTC drug-facts and menthol/camphor labeling counsel.
- Shipping and returns policy.

Do not add disease, medical, pain-outcome, analgesic, anti-inflammatory,
anti-irritant, vasodilator, or therapeutic claims to storefront or ad copy.
Formula-source function notes are internal reference material, not marketing
claims.
